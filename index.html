What is an ATAR?

The Australian Tertiary Admission Rank is a rank, not a mark out of 100. It runs from 0 to 99.95 and shows where a student stands against everyone their age in their state.

An ATAR of 80 means you did better than 80% of your age group. Universities publish the lowest ATAR they accepted for each course.
